# Requests, by mode

Plain words work; the skill asks for what it needs (the task, the volume, the budget) one question at a time, with a proposed answer. These show what each mode covers.

## Quick check: is it worth a closer look

```text
Is this tool legit? https://example.com
```

```text
Fifteen minutes on this AI headshot app before I sign up: what runs underneath, and what do I give away on the free plan?
```

## Check: one service, for a task

```text
I want to generate 3D models for our product pages, about 40 a month, GLB under 10 MB for a web viewer. Check this generator for that before I pay.
```

```text
We need 30 vertical 10-second ads a month for clients. Check this AI video studio for commercial use, watermark and what a month really costs.
```

```text
We're about to upload client contracts to this assistant. Check where the data goes, whether it trains on it and how long it keeps it.
```

## Compare: two to four services, the same test set

```text
Compare these three image generators for product photos of our candles: quality on my five photos, commercial rights and the cost of 200 images a month.
```

## Is it the model they sell

```text
This studio sells Seedance 2.0. Here are two clips I made there. Are they consistent with Seedance?
```

```text
This assistant says it runs on Opus. Check whether it behaves like Opus or like a smaller model; tell me first how many runs that takes and what it costs.
```

## Money and the vendor's price

```text
Is the yearly plan of this aggregator a good deal against paying the model vendor directly, for about 50 videos a month?
```

## Terms only

```text
Read the terms and privacy policy of this service before we upload our product photos: ownership, training, retention, refunds, cancellation.
```

## Mobile apps

```text
Check this iPhone AI photo app before I subscribe: who's behind it, what the subscription costs per year and where my photos go.
```

## In other languages

The agent answers in the language you write in:

```text
Можно ли пользоваться этим сервисом? Думаю взять годовую подписку, делаю короткие ролики для соцсетей.
```

```text
Сравни эти два 3D-генератора для ювелирки: качество на моих трёх фото, права на модели и цена 30 моделей в месяц.
```
