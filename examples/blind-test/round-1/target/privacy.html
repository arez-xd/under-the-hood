Privacy Policy

1. Controller

Northwind Apps Ltd, Limassol, Cyprus, is the controller of your personal data.

2. What we collect

Account data, payment data processed by our payment provider, prompts, uploaded images ("Inputs") and generated videos ("Outputs").

3. How we use your data

We may use Inputs and Outputs to train, evaluate and improve our models and the models of our providers. Enterprise customers may opt out of training by contacting their account manager.

4. Sharing

We share Inputs with third-party AI model providers to generate Outputs, and with hosting and analytics providers.

5. Retention

We keep Inputs and Outputs for as long as your account exists and for 24 months after it is closed.
