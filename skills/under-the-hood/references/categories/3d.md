# Profile: 3D generators

Text-to-3D and image-to-3D services, retopology and texturing tools. A model that looks good in a turntable can be unusable: a million triangles, no UVs, textures baked with lighting, the wrong scale.

## Before running

Ask what the model is for; the bar depends on it:

| Use | Triangles | Textures | Other |
|---|---|---|---|
| Web viewer, product page, AR | under ~50–100k | PBR, 1–2k, compressed | GLB or USDZ, real-world scale, under ~10 MB |
| Game asset | budget per asset class | PBR, consistent texel density | clean quads or good triangles, UVs without heavy overlap, pivot and scale |
| 3D print | any, but watertight | not needed | manifold, wall thickness, flat base, STL or 3MF |
| Concept and blockout | not important | optional | speed and fidelity to the reference |

## What to measure

Run `node scripts/glb-inspect.mjs <file.glb> --budget-tris <n> --budget-mb <n> --max-texture <px>` on every result. It reports:

- triangles and vertices, meshes and primitives;
- bounding box size (glTF units are metres): is the object 2 cm or 2 km;
- UV sets and normals present; vertex colours instead of textures;
- materials and their PBR channels (base colour, metallic-roughness, normal, occlusion, emissive);
- textures: count, format, pixel size, bytes;
- compression extensions (Draco, meshopt, KTX2) and what the file requires to load;
- file size, and what exceeds the budget.

Then look, in a viewer that shows wireframe and channels (the service's own, or any glTF viewer):

- **topology:** dense noise, holes, floating fragments, inverted normals;
- **lighting baked into the base colour** (shadows and highlights painted on: the model can't be relit);
- **back and underside:** image-to-3D often invents them;
- **thin parts:** handles, straps, text, hands;
- **symmetry and proportions** against the input.

## Export and rights

- Formats offered per plan (GLB, FBX, OBJ, USDZ, STL), and whether the export changes the mesh (remesh, decimation) or the textures (resize).
- Whether free-plan results are public or carry a license with conditions: read the plan comparison and the terms (layer 6).
- Whether the result can be downloaded in bulk, through the API, and after the subscription ends.

## Tells from the result

`glb-inspect.mjs` prints the `asset.generator` field and any `extras` the exporter left; they often name the tool or the pipeline. Texture images can carry metadata too: run `media-meta.mjs` on extracted textures with `--extract` from `glb-inspect.mjs`.
